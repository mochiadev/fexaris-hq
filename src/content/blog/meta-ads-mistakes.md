## Introduction

You're spending money on Meta Ads. But are you getting customers? Or just engagement?

Let me ask you something: How many Ghanaian businesses have you seen with **great-looking ads but zero sales**?

That's exactly what happens when you make these three common mistakes.

---

## Mistake #1: The "Spray and Pray" Audience

**The Problem**: Targeting everyone because "maybe someone will buy."

```
❌ Bad: Women aged 18-65 in Ghana
✅ Good: Women aged 28-45 in Accra, interested in e-commerce, income $500+/month
```

### Ghana-Specific Fix
- **Location precision**: Target specific cities/neighborhoods
- **Behavior targeting**: Look for people who shop online
- **Custom audiences**: Retarget website visitors

---

## Mistake #2: Beautiful Ad, Wrong Objective

**The Problem**: Optimizing for "likes" when you want sales.

| Objective | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Awareness** | New brand launch |
| **Traffic** | Drive to website |
| **Conversions** | Sales, sign-ups, quotes |

### The Fix
- Use **Conversion objective** for sales
- Set up **Facebook Pixel** correctly
- Track key actions (purchase, add to cart, lead)

---

## Mistake #3: No Follow-Up Strategy

**The Problem**: Sending traffic to a landing page that does nothing.

**80% of clicks go to dead-end pages** that:
- Don't match the ad promise
- Are slow to load
- Have no clear CTA

### Ghana Reality Check
Your landing page must:
- Load in under 3 seconds (mobile)
- Show proof (reviews, testimonials)
- Make the next step OBVIOUS

---

## Quick Fix Checklist

- [ ] Narrow audience to 3-5 specific segments
- [ ] Use Conversion objective for sales
- [ ] Create dedicated landing page for each ad
- [ ] Add customer reviews to landing page
- [ ] Add WhatsApp button (Ghana prefers WhatsApp!)
