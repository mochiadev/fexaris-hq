## How to Get Started (No Coding Required)

### Step 1: Choose Your Platform

| Platform | Best For | Cost |
|----------|----------|------|
| **WhatsApp Business API** | Customer service | Free |
| **ManyChat** | Facebook Messenger | Free-$50/mo |
| **Tidio** | Website chat | Free-$25/mo |
| **Chatfuel** | Facebook bots | Free-$49/mo |

### Step 2: Define Your Use Case

Start with **one specific problem**:
- "I get 50+ 'Do you deliver?' messages daily"
- "I miss leads after hours"
- "My calendar is always full but I want more meetings"

### Step 3: Build Simple Flows

Start basic:
```
User message → Keyword match → Pre-written response
```

Example:
```
User: "Do you deliver to Kumasi?"
Bot: "Yes! We deliver to Kumasi within 2-3 business days. Would you like to see our delivery policy?"
```

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Being too generic | Use your brand voice |
| Not having an escape hatch | Always include "Talk to human" option |
| Overpromising | Be clear about what it can and can't do |
| Forgetting mobile users | Test on phone first |
