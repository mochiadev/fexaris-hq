## Introduction

Global SEO guides often miss something critical for African e-commerce:

**Local intent is different.**

When a Ghanaian searches "shoes," they might mean:
- "Shoes in Accra" (local)
- "Cheap shoes Ghana" (price-local)
- "Best shop for sneakers Accra" (specific)

This is your **competitive advantage** - global brands can't rank for these local terms.

---

## The African E-commerce SEO Advantages

### 1. Lower Competition for Local Terms

| Term | US Competition | Ghana Competition |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| "SEO agency" | High | Medium |
| "SEO in Accra" | N/A | Low |
| "Web design Ghana" | Low | Very Low |

**Action**: Focus on location-modified keywords.

### 2. Mobile-First Optimization = Better Ranking

Google prioritizes mobile-friendly sites. Most Ghanaian e-commerce sites are **not** mobile-optimized.

**Action**: If your site loads in under 3 seconds on mobile, you win.

### 3. WhatsApp Integration = Higher Engagement

**70% of Ghanaian shoppers prefer WhatsApp** for customer service.

**Action**: Add WhatsApp button to every product page.

## Quick Wins for African E-commerce SEO

### 1. Product Title Format

```
❌ Bad: "Premium Cotton T-Shirt"
✅ Good: "Premium Cotton T-Shirt - Accra Delivery, Cash on Delivery"
```

### 2. Image Optimization

- **Name files correctly**: `red-sneakers-accra.jpg` not `IMG_1234.jpg`
- **Add alt text**: "Red sneakers for delivery in Accra"
- **Compress**: Use TinyPNG to under 200KB

### 3. Local Schema Markup

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Your Store",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "addressLocality": "Accra",
    "addressCountry": "GH"
  }
}
```

## Google Business Profile for E-commerce

Even if you only sell online, GBF helps:

1. **"Near me" searches** - People search for stores near them
2. **Builds trust** - Visible business = legitimate
3. **Reviews** - Social proof for products

**Tip**: Create a "service area" listing if you deliver nationwide.
