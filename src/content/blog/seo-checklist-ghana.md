SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the **long-term growth strategy** that keeps giving back.

Unlike paid ads that stop when you stop paying, SEO continues driving traffic to your website **indefinitely** - even when you're sleeping.

## The Ghana SEO Reality

Here's what makes Ghana SEO unique:

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Mobile-first usage | 85% of searches are on mobile |
| Local intent | "Near me" searches dominate |
| Language mix | English + local languages |
| Connectivity | Many users on slow networks |

This means **global SEO advice often doesn't work** for Ghana.

---

## Your Ghana SEO Checklist

### Phase 1: Technical Foundation

- [ ] Mobile-responsive design (test on real phone)
- [ ] Page load time under 3 seconds (use GTmetrix)
- [ ] HTTPS security certificate
- [ ] Clean URL structure (use keywords)
- [ ] XML sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] robots.txt file configured correctly

### Phase 2: On-Page Optimization

- [ ] Page title under 60 characters
- [ ] Meta description under 160 characters
- [ ] H1 tag on every page (only one)
- [ ] Headers (H2, H3) for content structure
- [ ] Keywords naturally in first 100 words
- [ ] Alt text on all images
- [ ] Internal links to related content

### Phase 3: Local SEO (Critical for Ghana)

- [ ] Google Business Profile created
- [ ] Business hours accurate
- [ ] Services listed
- [ ] Photos uploaded (10+)
- [ ] Collect 5+ reviews
- [ ] NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)
- [ ] Local keywords (e.g., "SEO in Accra" not just "SEO")
- [ ] Directory listings (Jumia, Yellow Pages Ghana)

### Phase 4: Content Strategy

- [ ] Blog published at least monthly
- [ ] Content answers real customer questions
- [ ] Content optimized for local search terms
- [ ] Images optimized (compressed)
- [ ] Content updated quarterly

### Phase 5: Off-Page Authority

- [ ] Business listed in local directories
- [ ] Quality backlinks (1+ per month)
- [ ] Social media profiles active
- [ ] Customer testimonials featured
